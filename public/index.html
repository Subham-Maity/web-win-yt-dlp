ℹ️ How Full Webpage Scraper Works
  • 1. Scan Whole Webpage: Paste any video gallery, creator, or listing URL and click Scan Webpage to discover all video links & thumbnails on the page at once.
  • 2. Select & Queue (1-by-1): Pick videos (or Select All) and queue them into BullMQ (Redis). Downloads run strictly one-by-one until finished — ideal for slow or bandwidth-limited websites.
  • 3. Background Play & Network Sniff: When each job starts, the background browser opens that video muted, captures the fresh stream URL (.m3u8 / .mpd / .mp4) + session headers from network traffic, closes the tab, and hands it to yt-dlp before tokens expire.
